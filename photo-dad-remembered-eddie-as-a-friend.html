Dad remembered Eddie as a friend.

When Eddie Van Halen died in 2020, Dad posted about what he was like to work with. He said Eddie treated the Warner Bros. promotion people well and respected the work they did for the band. Dad had known those guys since the early Warner Bros. days, and that was what he wanted people to remember about Eddie.
