Dad played guitar with Billy Gibbons.

This photo was taken in Dad's Atlanta office. The story he tells happened backstage at a ZZ Top show in Savannah. Dad was resting on some equipment cases when Billy came over. Dad joked that he was learning blues licks, so Billy handed him one of his white stage guitars and invited him to warm up. Dad was nervous enough to struggle with an E chord, but Billy kept going until they found a song they could play together. Dad even got in a lead break. He writes about the encounter in Breaking Records (and Beating the Odds).
