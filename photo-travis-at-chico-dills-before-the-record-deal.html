Travis at Chico Dills, before the record deal.

This is Travis Tritt playing at Chico Dills on Sandy Plains Road in Marietta. Dad writes that it was the first time he saw Travis sing in public. They'd already met through Dad's studio, and the years of demos, phone calls and work toward a record deal were ahead of them. The photograph is credited to Debo Townsend McCurry in Dad's book.
