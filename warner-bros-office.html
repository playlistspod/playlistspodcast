03 / The Warner Bros. years

The office that felt bigger than life.

I loved visiting Dad at work. Mostly because of the walls.

From Ashley's memories

The walls were covered.

Dad worked in promotion, so his office at Warner Bros. was covered with posters and promotional material for whatever records were coming out next. (check out that James Taylor Walking Man poster behind him in this pic!) I loved going there. It always felt a little bigger than life, and I could spend forever staring at everything around me.

Some of those posters ended up at home, too. Dad used them to cover the drywall in his studio.

I love this picture of Mom and Dad in his office.

From the file cabinet · Record World · September 18, 1976 · p. 6

A thank-you from Fleetwood Mac.

In this 1976 Record World feature, Fleetwood Mac thanked the Warner Bros. promotion team with personally signed album-cover enlargements. Dad is pictured in the front row, holding one of them. We still have that signed piece in the family.
